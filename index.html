The focused scope

Four screens, with every second doing a job.

React Native Expo Go preview Reanimated motion iPhone + Android Light haptics 25-minute timer

  1. 01

    Splash

    A locked camera on a deep night field. A soft gold orb with a dark core breathes once over four seconds, swelling, holding, then settling before easing into Home. No copy, letters, buttons, or still-image shortcut.

  2. 02

    Home

    The same orb continues a slow breath behind a 25-minute timer. Start and pause work for real, the ring shows time remaining, and starting a session gives one light haptic response.

  3. 03

    Done

    When the timer reaches zero, the orb breaks into gold particles and one comet crosses the night field over roughly three seconds. Then: a restrained “Session complete” and a way back to Home.

  4. 04

    Settings

    Haptics on or off, plus a daily goal. Nothing else competing for attention.

Out of scope by design: sign-in, onboarding, profiles, journals, feeds, and extra screens.

Questions, answered plainly

About the Lumen Drift build.

Will this run in Expo Go? +

Yes. The planned delivery is an Expo Go preview so the experience can be opened and tested on a phone during the build process.

Is this meant to become a full productivity app? +

Not in this scope. Lumen Drift is a focused timer product. The purpose is to make the core ritual convincing before adding any broader product ideas.

How will the splash and completion motion be built? +

The motion is planned in Reanimated so it remains smooth on-device. The opening breath, timer atmosphere, particle burst, and comet are part of the interaction, not static screens.

What is deliberately excluded? +

There is no sign-in, onboarding sequence, profile, journal, social layer, or extra navigation. The build stays at the scale described: Splash, Home, Done, and Settings.
